Junior Data Analyst
Nordwind Commerce
Location: Remote (Europe, Middle East and Africa)
Employment type: Full time
Level: Entry-level

About the role
Nordwind Commerce runs online shops in twelve countries. You will join the analytics team and help store managers understand their weekly sales.

Responsibilities
- Build and update weekly sales dashboards
- Clean and combine data from the shop and warehouse systems
- Present findings to store managers in short reports

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in economics, statistics, computer science or a similar field (required)
- SQL for reporting (required)
- Python or R for data analysis (required)
- At least 1 year of professional experience in data analysis (required)
- Fluent English (required)
- Power BI (nice to have)
- German at B2 level (nice to have)
